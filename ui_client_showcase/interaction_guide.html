SYSTEM INTERACTION ARCHITECTURE • CLIENT SPECIFICATION

End-to-End User Interaction & Real-Time Data Flows

A deep dive into how customers, wholesale partners, and warehouse operators interact with the platform. This document traces live WebSocket triggers, AI copilot formula streaming, dynamic shopping cart recalculations, and warehouse IoT telemetry.

🛒 1. Customer Shopping & Instant Cart Interaction

B2C & B2B STOREFRONT
STEP 01

Discovery & Filter

Customer arrives on the Store tab, filters by category (Ovens, Mixers, Flours), and searches with instant client-side query matching.

EVENT: CategoryFilterChanged
STEP 02

Spec Modal Inspection

Clicking an item opens the modal portal showing power voltage, dimensions, chamber capacity, and live inventory count.

EVENT: OpenProductModal(id)
STEP 03

Quantity & Add to Cart

Customer chooses quantity and clicks 'Add to Cart'. The slide-over drawer animates open immediately showing updated totals.

STATE: Zustand useCartStore.add
STEP 04

Freight & Checkout

The free freight progress bar computes remaining amount. Entering coupon codes triggers instant discounts with celebratory confetti.

ANIMATION: Canvas Confetti Burst
🔄 Store & Cart State Interaction Sequence
  • Customer ➔ Selects "Heavy Deck Oven 4-Tray" and sets quantity to 2
  • Product Modal ➔ Dispatches addToCart(product, 2) to Zustand Global Store
  • Cart Drawer ➔ Recalculates Subtotal ($6,800.00), Tax ($544.00), and validates Free Freight Threshold (> $2,500)
  • Checkout Action ➔ Applies Coupon BAKE10 (-10% Discount) and renders confirmation celebration

✨ 2. AI Baking Concierge & Formula Interaction

SIGNALR STREAMING AGENT
STEP 01

Drawer Activation

Baker opens the AI Concierge from any screen to solve dough hydration, oven steaming, or fermentation issues.

UI: Slide-Over AI Drawer
STEP 02

Formula Inquiry

User enters questions like: "Calculate 78% hydration sourdough for 4 loaves with whole wheat blend".

WEBSOCKET: /agentHub SendMessage
STEP 03

Formula Computation

The AI formats exact Baker's Percentages (Flour: 2,000g, Water: 1,560g, Starter: 400g, Salt: 40g).

STREAM: Live Token Streaming
STEP 04

Direct Product Link

AI attaches recommended commercial proofing baskets and bannetons directly into chat with 1-click cart insertion.

CTA: 1-Click Product Injection

📦 3. Warehouse Operations & Telemetry Interaction

INDUSTRIAL IOT & LOGISTICS
STEP 01

Live Telemetry Feed

Sensor gauges monitor cold fermentation rooms (4.2°C) and grain storage humidity (62%) in real-time.

SIGNAL: IoT Sensor Telemetry
STEP 02

Stock Threshold Alert

When deck ovens or organic rye flour fall below safety limits, the system raises visual low-stock badges.

ALERT: Stock < Threshold
STEP 03

1-Click Restock PO

Warehouse managers click 'Trigger Restock PO' to instantly notify certified European manufacturers.

ACTION: Automated PO Generation
STEP 04

Fulfillment Tracker

Tracks commercial orders from Pick & Pack -> Freight Loading -> En Route with live GPS milestones.

STATUS: Dispatch Telemetry
